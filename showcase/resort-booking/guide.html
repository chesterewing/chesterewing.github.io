Your website, made simple

You don't need any tech skills. This guide shows the few easy steps to get your booking website live, and how to run everything from your phone.

Part 1

Getting your website live

One time only. We do the technical parts — you just prepare and approve.

Send your details

Message us with: your resort name, logo (a clear photo is fine), 4–8 good photos, your rates, GCash number or QR, phone number, Facebook or Messenger link, and your address.

Pick your web address

Something like yourresort.com. Don't have one yet? We'll help you choose and register it — about ₱600–1,000 a year, in your name, so it stays yours.

Pay the downpayment to start

Once we both agree on the plan, a downpayment starts the work. We set everything up for you.

Review your preview

We send you a private link to check everything — your photos, prices and colors. Tell us anything you want changed. We fix it.

Go live

We connect your web address, hand you your login, and do a short walkthrough call. Your website is now online for everyone.

That's it. From here, you run everything from your phone — no computer needed.
Part 2

Running it from your phone

Open your website and add /admin at the end (like yourresort.com/admin), or open the installed app. Sign in with your email and password.

✅ Approve a booking

  1. New bookings appear on the Today screen under "Paid, waiting for you."
  2. Tap the payment screenshot to see it bigger. Check your GCash for that exact amount.
  3. Tap Approve — the date is now locked for that guest. (Or Decline to free the date.)

🏖️ On the day guests arrive

  • Arriving today shows who's coming. When they arrive, open their booking and tap Check in.
  • Guests can show a QR code from their booking page — scanning it opens their details instantly.

📞 Add a phone or walk-in booking

  • Tap Add booking, fill in the date, package, name and number. It's confirmed right away and blocks the date so no one books over it.

💱 Change your prices

  • Go to Rates, tap a package, change the weekday or weekend price, and tap Save. New prices apply to new bookings.

📷 Add or change photos

  • Go to Photos, tap Add photos (from your camera or gallery). Reorder them; the first photo becomes your cover.

👥 Add your staff

  • Go to More → Staff → Add a staff account. Give each person their own email and password, so the history shows who approved each booking.

⚙️ Change your info anytime

  • Go to Settings to update your GCash, contact details, logo, colors, terms and more. It updates on your website right away.
Part 3

Install it as an app

So it opens with one tap, like any other app.

AndroidOpen your website, tap the menu, choose "Install app" — or open the APK we give you.
iPhone / iPadOpen it in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen."
Windows / MacOpen it in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar.

Ang website mo, pinadaling-dali

Hindi mo kailangang marunong sa computer. Ipapakita ng gabay na ito ang ilang madaling hakbang para mapa-live ang booking website mo, at kung paano patakbuhin lahat gamit ang phone mo.

Bahagi 1

Pagpapa-live ng website mo

Isang beses lang. Kami ang bahala sa mga teknikal — ikaw, maghanda at mag-aprub lang.

Ipadala ang detalye mo

I-message mo kami ng: pangalan ng resort, logo (OK na ang malinaw na larawan), 4–8 magagandang larawan, presyo mo, GCash number o QR, telepono, Facebook o Messenger link, at address mo.

Pumili ng web address

Tulad ng resortmo.com. Wala ka pa? Tutulungan ka naming pumili at magparehistro — mga ₱600–1,000 kada taon, nakapangalan sa iyo, kaya sa iyo talaga ito.

Magbayad ng downpayment para magsimula

Kapag sang-ayon na tayo sa plano, ang downpayment ang magsisimula ng trabaho. Kami na ang bahala sa buong setup.

Tingnan ang preview mo

Padadalhan ka namin ng pribadong link para suriin ang lahat — larawan, presyo, at kulay. Sabihin mo lang kung may papalitan. Aayusin namin.

I-live na

Ikokonekta namin ang web address mo, ibibigay ang login mo, at may maikling walkthrough call. Online na ang website mo para sa lahat.

Ayun lang. Mula dito, phone mo lang ang gagamitin mo — walang computer na kailangan.
Bahagi 2

Pagpapatakbo gamit ang phone mo

Buksan ang website mo at dagdagan ng /admin sa dulo (hal. resortmo.com/admin), o buksan ang naka-install na app. Mag-sign in gamit ang email at password mo.

✅ Mag-aprub ng booking

  1. Lumalabas ang bagong booking sa Today screen sa ilalim ng "Paid, waiting for you."
  2. I-tap ang screenshot ng bayad para lumaki. Tingnan sa GCash mo kung tama ang eksaktong halaga.
  3. I-tap ang Approve — naka-lock na ang petsa para sa guest na iyon. (O Decline para buksan ulit ang petsa.)

🏖️ Sa araw na darating ang guests

  • Makikita sa Arriving today kung sino ang darating. Pagdating nila, buksan ang booking at i-tap ang Check in.
  • Puwedeng magpakita ang guest ng QR code mula sa booking page nila — i-scan mo para bumukas agad ang detalye nila.

📞 Magdagdag ng phone o walk-in booking

  • I-tap ang Add booking, ilagay ang petsa, package, pangalan at number. Kumpirmado agad at haharang sa petsa para walang makapatong na iba.

💱 Palitan ang presyo mo

  • Pumunta sa Rates, i-tap ang package, palitan ang weekday o weekend price, at i-tap ang Save. Para sa bagong booking ang bagong presyo.

📷 Magdagdag o magpalit ng larawan

  • Pumunta sa Photos, i-tap ang Add photos (mula sa camera o gallery). Ayusin ang pagkakasunod; ang unang larawan ang magiging cover.

👥 Magdagdag ng staff

  • Pumunta sa More → Staff → Add a staff account. Bigyan ang bawat isa ng sariling email at password, para makita sa history kung sino ang nag-aprub ng bawat booking.

⚙️ Palitan ang info mo kahit kailan

  • Pumunta sa Settings para baguhin ang GCash, contact, logo, kulay, terms, at iba pa. Agad itong nag-a-update sa website mo.
Bahagi 3

I-install bilang app

Para isang tap lang bumukas, parang kahit anong app.

iPhone / iPadBuksan sa Safari, i-tap ang Share, tapos "Add to Home Screen."
Windows / MacBuksan sa Chrome o Edge, i-click ang install icon sa address bar.
