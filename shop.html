FAQ

Before you buy

Do I need to know how to code?
No. I do the setup, and anything you change day to day (prices, services, hours, bookings) happens through normal screens and forms. If you get stuck, message me.
What do I need to prepare?
Your business name, logo (a phone photo is fine to start), a few good photos, your price list, contact details and social links. If you already own a domain, the login for where you bought it.
Where is my data saved?
The demos save to the browser you’re using, which is perfect for one person on one computer. If you need staff on different devices, or customers sending in bookings and orders, add Online accounts & database and everything is saved online.
Will someone else have the same website?
The layout is shared with other buyers. Your branding, content and data are yours alone, and nobody else can see your records. Want something nobody else has? That’s a custom build.
Are there costs after I buy?
Your domain renews yearly with the registrar. Hosting for these sites is often free or low-cost, and I’ll tell you the exact amount before setup. The care plan is optional.
How do I pay?
50% upfront, 50% on launch. GCash or bank transfer in the Philippines, PayPal or Wise for international clients. A receipt is sent for every payment.
Can you add or change a feature?
Yes. Small changes like wording, sections or colors are part of setup. Bigger additions get a quote before I start, so there are no surprises.
What if something breaks after launch?
Fixes are free for the first two weeks. After that, they’re covered by the care plan, or paid per task if you don’t have one.
