Before you ask

What actually changes the price

Page count is the least useful way to price a site. These are the things that genuinely move a quote, roughly in order of impact:

Who updates it
The single biggest factor. If you only ever need me to make changes, the site can stay simple and cheap. If you want to add posts, projects or products yourself, that needs an editable system behind it, usually a tier up.
Content
Text, photos and logo ready to go keep it at the quoted price. If I need to write copy, source images or design a logo, that’s separate work.
Design
Adapting a clean layout to your brand is quick. A design built from scratch around your idea takes longer, and I’ll say so before we start.
Integrations
Payments, booking, email automation, maps, analytics. Each one is real work and gets priced on its own.
Accounts
The moment people need to log in, you have roles, passwords, resets and security to think about. That’s Pro territory.
Heavy media
Video especially. Put showreels on YouTube or Vimeo and I’ll embed them. Free, fast, and they work on phones. Hosting large video files directly costs real money in bandwidth and I’ll quote it separately.
Deadline
I have a weekday job, so freelance happens evenings and weekends. Normal timelines are in the tiers above; a rush start is +30%.

FAQ

Before you reach out

Can you do both frontend and backend?
Yes. Most of my freelance work is full-stack: I’ll build the backend (APIs, database, auth), wire it to a frontend, and ship the whole thing. If you only need one half, I can scope just that.
Do you build mobile apps?
Native iOS / Android: no, that’s not my main lane. Mobile-first responsive web apps that work great on phones: yes, that’s what I do daily. If you want a “real app feel” I can also build a PWA (installable to home screen, works offline).
How do I pay? Do you take partial payment?
For projects above the Starter tier, I usually do 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. Payment via bank transfer or GCash (PH), or PayPal / Wise for international clients. Receipt sent for every payment.
Do you offer maintenance after launch?
Three options: (1) small monthly retainer for updates, bug fixes and hosting checks, (2) pay-per-task for occasional changes, (3) zero: you get the source, deploy yourself, and run it forever. Your call.
How fast can you start?
Depends on current capacity. See the meter at the top of this page. Typical: 1–2 weeks from first message to project kickoff. Rush starts cost +30%.
Do you work full-time on freelance projects?
No. I have a full-time backend role at Cebuanas Encoding Services. Freelance happens evenings and weekends, which is why turnarounds are stated in days and weeks, not hours. Be upfront about your deadline and I’ll be upfront about whether I can hit it.
What if I’m not happy with the result?
Each tier includes 1–3 rounds of revisions. If we hit a deeper disagreement, we talk it through. I want repeat clients, not refunded ones. Significant scope changes outside the original brief are quoted separately.
Can you teach my team along the way?
Yes. I’ll write a handover doc, do a 30–60 minute walkthrough call, and answer questions for about two weeks after launch at no extra cost. Beyond that, longer mentorship is a separate retainer.
What kinds of projects do you turn down?
Anything illegal or sketchy, anything that requires me to clone someone else’s product or brand, and projects where the budget doesn’t match the scope (I’d rather say no than rush). I’ll tell you straight if I’m the wrong fit.
Can I see examples of your work?
Yes. The Work page has project case studies and ready-made demos you can click into, and About covers what I’ve done in each role. RocketServe (live) is the biggest thing I’m currently building the backend for.
What if my project doesn’t fit any of these services?
Send me the brief anyway. If I’m the wrong fit, I’ll say so up front (and sometimes recommend someone better). If I’m partially the right fit, we’ll scope just the part that matches.
I just have a question first. Is that ok?
Of course. Discovery calls are free and non-pushy. Send a message with what you’re thinking and we’ll go from there.
