My story

Hardware habits, software job

I trained as a Computer Engineer, which means my first instinct around a problem is to ask what it costs — in memory, in queries, in the three a.m. phone call when something fails. Embedded work gives you finite resources and no easy retries. That turns out to fix a lot of bad software habits.

Four and a half years as a data encoder taught me something less glamorous and probably more valuable: that most systems fail at the boring edges. Bad input, a missing validation, a report nobody reconciled. I write backends like someone who has been on the receiving end of a broken one.

Now I build the backend behind RocketServe — APIs, auth, schema, and the business logic that moves jobs between process-serving agencies and the servers who work them.
