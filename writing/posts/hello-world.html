Hello, World — Why I Started This Site

November 12, 2025 · 3 min read · meta · portfolio

I've been writing code professionally for a few years now and never had a real place online to point to. Resumes are flat. LinkedIn is fine but feels like a CV with extra steps. So I rebuilt this site — partly as a portfolio, partly as a personal lab.

What I want this to do

  • Show, not just tell. Live RocketServe link, real project pages, public showcase of websites I build.
  • Stay easy to update. Manifest-driven pages (Tools, Showcase, Writing, Reviews) so adding a new entry is one JSON tweak, not an HTML rewrite.
  • Feel like me. Themes, dark mode, a small dev-aesthetic logo — small touches matter on a portfolio.

What's next

More writing here — short, useful, the kind of post I wish I'd found while debugging something obscure. A few real testimonials once I ship more freelance work. And case-study pages for the projects worth deeper context.

If you've got feedback or want to work together, drop me a line.
